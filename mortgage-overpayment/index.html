Questions

Will overpaying cost me a fee?

Most UK fixed-rate deals let you overpay up to 10% of the balance a year without an early repayment charge. The planner warns you if your plan goes over. Check your own mortgage offer for the exact rule.

Should I overpay or save?

Many people keep an emergency fund and clear more expensive debt first. The planner shows what overpaying saves, so you can compare it with what your savings earn.
