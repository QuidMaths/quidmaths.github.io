Questions

What's a sinking fund?

Money you put aside a little at a time for a cost you know is coming, so you can pay it without touching your everyday budget or a credit card.

Where should I keep the money?

Many people use a separate savings pot so it's not spent by accident. The planner tells you how much to move each month.
