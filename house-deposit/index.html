Questions

Who can open a Lifetime ISA?

Anyone aged 18 to 39 in the UK. You can pay in until you're 50. Check GOV.UK for the current rules.

What about Scotland and Wales?

They have their own property taxes (LBTT and LTT), so the stamp duty figure is for England and Northern Ireland. The rest of the planner works anywhere in the UK.

No. It's a calculator to help you plan, using published UK rates. Check GOV.UK or an adviser for your own situation.
