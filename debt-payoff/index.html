Why it matters

Paying the minimum on everything feels responsible. It's also the slowest, most expensive way out.

The problem isn't effort. It's that the money is spread thin across every debt, so none of them shrink fast, and the interest keeps topping them back up.

This planner puts every spare pound on one debt at a time, rolls each cleared payment onto the next, and shows you the exact month you'll be debt-free.

Questions

Avalanche or snowball, which is better?

Avalanche pays the highest interest rate first and saves the most money. Snowball clears the smallest balance first, which gives quicker wins. The planner lets you try both and see the difference.

What if I can only afford the minimums?

It still shows your debt-free date and the interest you'll pay. If debt is getting on top of you, StepChange (0800 138 1111) and MoneyHelper give free, confidential help.

How do I get my spreadsheet?

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. It opens on a "Start Here" tab. You type in the yellow cells and everything else works itself out. There are example rows so you can see what goes where.

Is this financial or tax advice?

No. It's a calculator to help you plan, using published UK rates. Check GOV.UK or an adviser for your own situation.
