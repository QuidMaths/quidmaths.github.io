Questions

Who can open a Lifetime ISA?

Anyone aged 18 to 39 in the UK. You can pay in until you're 50. Check GOV.UK for the current rules.

What about Scotland and Wales?

They have their own property taxes (LBTT and LTT), so the stamp duty figure is for England and Northern Ireland. The rest of the planner works anywhere in the UK.

How do I get my spreadsheet?

Straight after paying you're taken to your download page, and Stripe emails you a receipt. Save the file, then open it in Excel or upload it to Google Sheets (free).

Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Yes. Checkout is handled securely by Stripe and accepts cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. It opens on a "Start Here" tab. You type in the yellow cells and everything else works itself out. There are example rows so you can see what goes where.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes, in the free Google Sheets or Excel apps, though it's easiest to set up on a computer.

Is this financial or tax advice?

No. It's a calculator to help you plan, using published UK rates. Check GOV.UK or an adviser for your own situation.
