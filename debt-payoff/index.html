Paying the minimum on everything feels responsible. It's also the slowest, most expensive way out.

Questions

Avalanche or snowball, which is better?

Avalanche pays the highest interest rate first and saves the most money. Snowball clears the smallest balance first, which gives quicker wins. The planner lets you try both and see the difference.

What if I can only afford the minimums?

It still shows your debt-free date and the interest you'll pay. If debt is getting on top of you, StepChange (0800 138 1111) and MoneyHelper give free, confidential help.
