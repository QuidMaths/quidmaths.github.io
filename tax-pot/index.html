Questions

Do I need to pay tax on my Vinted or eBay sales?

Selling your own unwanted things usually isn't taxed. Buying to resell, or making things to sell, is trading. If your trading income is over £1,000 in a tax year, you need to tell HMRC. The spreadsheet checks this for you.

Does it work if I also have a job?

Yes. Type in your job pay once and it works out the extra tax on top, in the right tax band.

What about Scotland?

The Income Tax rates are for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Scotland's bands are different, so treat the Income Tax figure as a guide there. National Insurance is the same.
