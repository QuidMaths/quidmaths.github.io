No. You type in each balance when you check it. No bank login, so your details stay private.

Will I pay tax on my savings interest?

Interest outside ISAs counts towards your Personal Savings Allowance: £1,000 for basic-rate taxpayers, £500 for higher-rate. The Tax Check tab estimates whether you'll go over.

No. It's a calculator to help you plan, using published UK rates. Check GOV.UK or an adviser for your own situation.
